Curious Minute Publisher Terms of Service

Last updated: September 24, 2026

1. About the app

Curious Minute Publisher ("the app") is a private automation tool used solely by its owner to create short educational videos and publish them to the owner's own YouTube channel, Curious Minute. It is not offered, sold or licensed to anyone else, and it has no public sign-up or user accounts.

2. YouTube terms

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app, the user (the channel owner) agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The app is operated in accordance with the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and the YouTube API Services Developer Policies. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Authorization and control

  • The app acts only on the YouTube channel that the owner explicitly authorized through Google OAuth, and only within the permissions listed on the home page.
  • The owner configures and enables automated uploads, including how many videos are published, when they are published and their privacy status (uploads are sent as private with a scheduled publish time). The owner can edit, unpublish or delete any video in YouTube Studio at any time.
  • The owner can pause the app at any time, and can revoke its access at Google security settings.

4. Content standards

  • Videos must comply with YouTube's Community Guidelines, copyright rules and monetization policies.
  • Scripts are based on cited public sources and are fact-checked before upload. Scripts that fail review are discarded.
  • Only openly licensed or original media is used, and image authors and licenses are credited in each video description.
  • Each video description discloses the use of AI-assisted scripting and a synthetic narrator voice. Realistic altered or synthetic content, if ever used, is declared through YouTube's altered-content setting.
  • The app avoids sensitive topics such as tragedies, violence, politics, medical or financial advice and adult content.

5. Privacy

How the app accesses, uses, stores and deletes data is described in the Privacy Policy.
